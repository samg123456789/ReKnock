## HOW IT WORKS

**Header:** Three steps. Ten seconds a quote.

**1. Log the quote.**
Forward the quote email, or type a name, number, and amount. That's it.

**2. ReKnock re-knocks.**
Four short texts over three weeks, written in your voice - not robot-speak. Day 2, day 5, day 10, day 20. Polite every time.

**3. They reply, you take over.**
The instant a homeowner answers, the texts stop. You get a clean email, the thread is waiting in the ReKnock inbox, and your reply goes back from your ReKnock number.

## FAQ

**Do homeowners know it's automated?**
The texts come from your dedicated business number and sound like you, because you approve every word before anything sends. When they reply, it IS you.

**What if someone tells me to stop texting?**
They reply STOP once and they're removed automatically, permanently. You stay on the right side of the law without thinking about it.

**I use Jobber / Housecall Pro / QuickBooks.**
Fine - keep them. ReKnock doesn't replace anything. Log the quote in ReKnock (or connect via Zapier) and we handle just the chasing.

**What trades does this work for?**
Any trade that sends a quote and waits: roofing, painting, fencing, decks, concrete, tree work, garage doors, landscaping. If homeowners ghost you, it works.

**How fast is setup?**
About 10 minutes: answer a few questions so the texts sound like you, approve your follow-ups, log your first quote. (Your dedicated texting number takes 1–2 business days to activate - carrier rules, not ours.)

**Can I see what it sent?**
Every message, every reply, every quote - one screen. Plus a monthly tally of replies you got and jobs you closed off followed-up quotes.
